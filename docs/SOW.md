# jojoCRM Statement of Work

Status: delivery agreement for the product in [PRD.md](PRD.md).

## Parties

This agreement is between the Product owner and Delivery. There is no outside client commissioning the build.

## Work

Delivery builds the jojoCRM product defined in [PRD.md](PRD.md), inside the boundary in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md), by completing the stories in [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md).

The build is the Django JSON API and the separate responsive web app described in the PRD. Sprint 8 is the public release of that full product.

Starter tables are the shared core plus the business-type pack chosen at workspace creation, as defined in [PRD.md](PRD.md). Every type has at least 10 tables. The agreed packs have 17.

## Commercial terms

There is no fee for the build.

The scope is the PRD. The schedule is eight two-week sprints, in the milestone order in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md). A missed checklist item carries into the next milestone. The calendar does not pause. The work is accepted when all fourteen PRD acceptance items pass at the end of sprint 8.

Prices a tenant pays to use jojoCRM are defined in the Billing section of [PRD.md](PRD.md): GHS per active seat, a 60-day card trial, card renewal, mobile-money prepay, and a separate SMS wallet. This agreement does not restate or change those prices.

## Prerequisites

Delivery integrates the providers listed in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md). The Product owner supplies the accounts and approvals named there before the sprint that depends on them. A sprint whose prerequisite is missing cannot be acceptance-tested for the dependent stories.

## Change control

The scope is frozen to the PRD. Delivery does not build a new request until a scope revision records the request, the PRD section it changes, the milestones it affects, and whether the remaining milestone dates stay or are rewritten. The revision updates the scope, this agreement, and the backlog before that work starts.

## Acceptance

Each milestone has the checklist in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md). The Product owner accepts the project once, at the end of sprint 8, against the fourteen acceptance items in the PRD.

## Exclusions

Work outside [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md), including the list under "Out of the product" in [PRD.md](PRD.md), is outside this agreement.
